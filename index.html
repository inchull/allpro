Insurance & questions

Your car. Your choice of shop.

In Georgia, you decide who repairs your vehicle. Your insurer can recommend a shop, but it can't make you use one. Tell your adjuster you're bringing it to All Pro Collision and we'll take it from there.

Start a claim estimate
Do I have to use the body shop my insurance company recommends?

No. In Georgia you choose where your vehicle is repaired. Your insurer can suggest shops in its network, but the decision is yours. Tell your adjuster you want the work done at All Pro Collision in Doraville and we will handle the estimate and paperwork with them.

How much does a collision repair estimate cost?

Estimates at All Pro Collision are free. Bring the vehicle by the shop in Doraville, or send photos through the estimate form and we will reply with a written, line-by-line estimate.

How long will my repair take?

It depends on the damage and on parts availability. A bumper or single-panel repair is often done in a few days. Larger collision repairs with structural work or back-ordered parts take longer. Your written estimate includes a target completion date, and we call you if anything changes.

What is a supplement on a body shop estimate?

A supplement is an addition to the original estimate for damage that could not be seen until the vehicle was taken apart. When we find hidden damage, we document it with photos and send it to you or your insurer for approval before doing the extra work.

Will the new paint match the rest of my car?

Yes. We mix paint to your vehicle's factory paint code, check it against the car itself, and blend into the adjacent panels so the repaired area does not stand out in sunlight.
